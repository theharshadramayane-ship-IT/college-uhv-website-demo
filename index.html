THE FOUNDATION OF RELATIONSHIPS

9 Core Human Values

"The essence of harmony lies in understanding these nine foundational feelings, guiding us from basic trust to universal love."

Trust (विश्वास)

The foundation of every bond — the assurance that the other person genuinely wishes for my wellbeing and growth.

Respect (सन्मान)

Recognizing every individual with fairness, acknowledging their unique potential and purpose in life.

Affection (स्नेह)

A natural sense of closeness, embracing others as one's own with warmth and belonging.

Care (ममता)

A heartfelt commitment to nurture and protect the physical wellbeing of those around us.

Guidance (वात्सल्य)

A sense of duty to guide others toward clarity and right understanding in every step.

Reverence (श्रद्धा)

Wholeheartedly acknowledging and honoring excellence and greatness found in others.

Glory (गौरव)

Celebrating the dedication and hard work of those who strive for excellence in life.

Gratitude (कृतज्ञता)

A deep appreciation for the efforts and contributions others make toward my growth and success.

Love (प्रेम)

The ultimate value — a boundless sense of connection with all human beings and nature itself.
